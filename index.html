FAQ

Quick answers before game night.

What is WHO'S LYING?

A social deduction party game where most players know the secret word and one hidden liar tries to blend in.

How do I join the beta?

Enter your email in the beta form at the top of this page. You'll receive beta and launch updates by email.

How do rooms work?

A host creates a room and shares the room code. Other players enter that code to join the match.

What happens when the liar is caught?

The liar gets one final guess at the secret word. A correct guess can steal the round.
